These are offline captures, not an FPS benchmark. The model is a GPU spectral ocean with a separate depth-averaged interaction field. Whitewater and secondary effects include approximations; this is not a full volumetric FLIP solver. No image generation, stock water footage, frame interpolation or upscaling was used.
