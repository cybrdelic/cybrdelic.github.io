The original bubble close-up was occluded by a scene rock and its deliberate emitter lay inside that rock. A dedicated bubble preset now places the source in open water and uses a clear sight line. Bubble births remain an explicit secondary-effects source, not resolved air entrainment.

Dispersive optical ring packets have 3.4 mm base amplitude, a 4096-event budget and a reactive temporal mask so fast-changing fine slopes do not inherit stale ocean history. These packets are a sub-grid visual model, not validated droplet-impact physics.
