Physics, not just a blue material.
The ocean uses three disjoint spectral bands, seeded Gaussian coefficients and the gravity-wave dispersion relation. Buoy heave is integrated from submerged volume, gravity and quadratic drag.
Choppy displacement, crest foam, spray emission and cloud lighting are approximations. The local interaction patch is a separate linear shallow-water model. This build does not resolve overturning, turbulent aeration or two-way fluid–solid coupling.
